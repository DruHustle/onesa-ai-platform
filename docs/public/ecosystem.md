# Onesa ecosystem

Onesa UG develops intelligent software, digital platforms and robotics. The Company website introduces the company and its products. Onesa AI Platform has Applications and Industrial AI tracks. Applications include the protected Chat, Knowledge, Agents and Travel workspace; Industrial Investigation is an isolated protected synthetic demonstration of evidence-backed investigation and human-reviewed drafts; live industrial integrations remain under development. Onesa Robot provides a protected operator console for supervised software simulation.

## Access and data boundaries

Products have separate sessions, permissions and private data. A product link does not grant access or single sign-on. Public documentation does not expose conversations, knowledge uploads, applicant records, governance information, Robot history or telemetry.

The Company website remains independent of AI inference. Models cannot bypass tool permissions or Robot motion validation. Hardware integration, autonomous vehicles and broader physical AI remain development directions unless a specific capability is expressly identified as available.

## Understand capability descriptions

Implemented software, tested simulation, live inference and connected hardware describe different states. Concept artwork is illustrative. Development or simulation evidence does not certify a physical system. Product pages and protected interfaces identify the relevant access and operating context.
