# Onesa ecosystem

Onesa UG develops intelligent software, digital platforms and robotics. The Company website introduces the company and its products. Onesa AI Platform is a general-purpose engine for applications and intelligent systems. Onesa AI Robot explores its use in the physical world and is under development.

## Familiar behavior, distinct purposes

The products share Onesa's visual foundations and familiar navigation while providing interfaces for their different tasks. The Company website presents product information, news, research and contact enquiries. The AI Platform provides conversations, Knowledge, Agents and application workflows. Robot provides a protected development and simulation workspace.

The same AI engine can support different industries. Recommendations and decision support include travel planning, business feasibility and investment research. Smart farming connects crop selection, cultivation planning, condition monitoring, adaptation and selling opportunities. These are application directions; a product description does not establish that every integration is available.

## Access and data boundaries

Products have separate sessions, permissions and private data. A product link does not grant access or single sign-on. Public documentation does not expose conversations, knowledge uploads, applicant records, governance information, Robot history or telemetry.

The Company website remains independent of AI inference. Models cannot bypass tool permissions or Robot motion validation. Hardware integration, autonomous vehicles and broader physical AI remain development directions unless a specific capability is expressly identified as available.

## Understand capability descriptions

Implemented software, tested simulation, live inference and connected hardware describe different states. Concept artwork is illustrative. Development or simulation evidence does not certify a physical system. Product pages and protected interfaces identify the relevant access and operating context.
