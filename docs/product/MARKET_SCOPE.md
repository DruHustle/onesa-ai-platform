# Market scope

Onesa AI is a European-based, general-purpose, modular AI platform for enterprise, developer,
institutional and consumer applications across European and international markets. Shared
self-hosted infrastructure enables organizations to operate and customize open-weight models.
European businesses, SMEs, institutions and research groups are priority commercial audiences;
architecture and data contracts do not restrict geography, industry or language.

English, German, French, Spanish and further European/international languages are evaluation
targets. Shona, Ndebele, other African languages and regional knowledge remain optional
specializations. None of these targets establishes verified model proficiency: this workspace
runs a labelled mock and has no real-model language baseline. Interface translations are separate.

Common opportunities: private assistants, enterprise document intelligence, industrial knowledge,
travel research and customizable inference APIs. Actual integrations, tenant/organization controls,
quality, infrastructure costs and commercial demand must be validated before offering a service.
No Africa-only identity, fixed-country routing, one-checkpoint-for-all strategy or separate regional
platform is required. The suggested tagline is draft copy, not an approved official brand asset.
